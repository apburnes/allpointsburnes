[cloud.gov Pages](https://cloud.gov/pages/) is a platform for publishing static websites quickly, securely, and with minimal operational overhead.

I have led this product from **2022 to the present**, helping shape the roadmap, guide implementation, and improve the overall developer and operator experience.

## Platform

cloud.gov Pages gives teams a managed path to deploy static sites without having to build and maintain their own hosting platform. It supports a modern publishing workflow while reducing the amount of infrastructure work required from delivery teams.

## Value proposition

The value of the platform is simple:

- teams can ship sites faster
- hosting is consistent and easier to operate
- the platform reduces repetitive infrastructure work
- open source foundations make the system more transparent and adaptable

## My role

As the lead on the product, I help drive product direction, technical decisions, platform improvements, and delivery priorities.
